About Kwetutech

Kwetutech is a youth-led initiative started by young Congolese. We make hands-on technology learning accessible and help young people build practical solutions for their communities.

We explore embedded systems through electronics, Arduino, microcontrollers, sensors, robotics, and the Internet of Things (IoT). Learners discover how the technology works by building and testing it themselves.

Workshops, shared learning, and mentorship help young people develop skills and confidence—and keep improving when a first attempt does not work.

What We Do

We experiment

We work with electronics, Arduino, and sensors, and we try things to see what happens.

We build

We turn ideas into working prototypes, even with limited resources.

We learn together

Young people mentor each other. When something fails, we find out why and improve it.

Our Impact

Our Story

Kwetutech began with a few young Congolese who wanted to understand how technology works by building it themselves. We started with small components, curiosity, and the decision to try.

Our Vision

We want every young Congolese who is curious about technology to have a place to build, to fail safely, and to improve—growing a generation of creators and problem-solvers.

Our Mission

We make hands-on technology learning accessible and help young people build practical solutions for their communities through experimentation, workshops, and shared learning.

Young people who had only heard about technology are now making it work with their own hands.

Limited resources became practical experiments, and experiments became real prototypes.
